Vengeance Complete

God of War III brings the epic Greek saga to its dramatic conclusion. Kratos, aided by the Titans, storms Mount Olympus to exact his revenge on the gods who betrayed him.

Armed with the Blades of Exile and an arsenal of devastating weapons, Kratos battles through the heights of Olympus and the depths of Hades to confront Zeus and end the reign of the Olympian Gods.

Experience the most brutal and epic battles in the series, featuring larger-than-life boss fights against Zeus, Poseidon, Hades, and more Olympian deities.

This is Kratos' final journey of vengeance - a path that will shake the very foundations of the world.
